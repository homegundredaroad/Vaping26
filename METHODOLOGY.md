# Methodology

Vaping26 separates collection, interpretation and publication.

## Evidence pipeline

1. Source registration
2. Collection or retrieval
3. Normalisation
4. Provenance capture
5. Validation
6. Cross-reference
7. Evidence classification
8. Publication review
9. Sanitisation and disclosure checks
10. Public release

## Health evidence

Health evidence is classified by study design and assessed for population, exposure definition, smoking history, dual use, duration, comparator, outcome, confounding, funding and limitations. Study design is not treated as an automatic truth score.

## Retail and product evidence

Retailer or product records may be observed, matched, unresolved or verified. Automated matching never establishes illegality or misconduct. Public statements of non-compliance require suitable authoritative evidence.

## Enforcement evidence

Enforcement events are attributed to the originating authority and kept distinct from allegations, media reports and automated signals.

## Reproducibility

Published datasets and reports should carry a generation timestamp, run identifier, source summary and cryptographic manifest where practical.
