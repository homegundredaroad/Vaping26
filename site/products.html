Product records are evidence sources, not compliance verdicts.

Vaping26 collects public regulatory product information and can reconcile product metadata, but a failed automated match does not mean a product is illegal and a successful match does not prove every item on sale is compliant.

Publication rule

Automated matching is a research aid. Vaping26 will not publish a claim of product illegality or non-compliance unless the underlying authoritative evidence supports that statement.
