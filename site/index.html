Research areas

The project separates distinct evidence questions instead of combining them into a single judgement about vaping.

Health

Scientific and clinical evidence

Bibliographic research, registered trials, smoking-cessation evidence, exposure, potential health effects and uncertainty.

Prevalence

Adult and youth trends

Official statistical resources and, after validation, structured trend analysis with survey context and limitations.

High street

Retail geography

Candidate retail locations, business records and geographic change. Presence in a dataset is not evidence of wrongdoing.

Products

Regulatory product records

Public product-register evidence and cautiously matched metadata, with product-level claims gated for human review.

Enforcement

Official enforcement evidence

Structured analysis of authoritative public releases. Media items remain discovery leads unless corroborated.

Regulation

Rules and policy

A developing timeline of legislation, official policy and measurable changes in the UK nicotine landscape.

Publication boundary

  • Raw/private investigative material is excluded from the public repository.
  • Association and causation are reported separately.
  • Automated matching does not establish illegality, misconduct or health causation.
  • Identifiable retailer, manufacturer or enforcement claims require appropriate evidence and human review.
  • Published files carry visible provenance and integrity metadata where available.

Source transparency

The public source catalogue will populate from the Research engine.

Vaping26 records source family, evidence role, implementation status and run-level coverage separately so that a failed or unconfigured source does not silently disappear.
