UK Vaping & Nicotine Observatory

Vaping26

Evidence before conclusion: health, prevalence, the high street, products, enforcement and regulation.

Project status

The observatory is being built. Public findings will be released only after provenance, validation and disclosure checks.

Research areas

Health

Clinical and scientific evidence, smoking cessation, exposure and uncertainty.

Prevalence

Adult and youth trends from official population surveys.

High street

Retail density, openings, closures and geographic change.

Products

Regulatory records, brands and product matching.

Enforcement

Official seizures, inspections, prosecutions and other enforcement activity.

Regulation

Changes in UK vaping and nicotine rules and their measurable effects.

Publication principle

No single source establishes causation, retailer misconduct, product illegality, regulatory non-compliance, criminality or health impact.
