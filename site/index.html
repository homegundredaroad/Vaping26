What do we actually know about vaping?

Vaping26 continuously collects, checks and organises scientific research and official UK data so benefits, harms, uncertainty and changing patterns can be examined separately rather than collapsed into a single claim.

How to cite Vaping26

Project, release and dataset citation guidance with explicit review-state caveats.

What the project will not do

  • Call vaping safe or harmful from publication counts.
  • Treat association as proof of causation.
  • Treat a company, shop or product match as evidence of illegality.
  • Allow AI to invent outcomes, effect estimates or risk-of-bias judgements.
  • Use satellite or ambient-air data as direct evidence that somebody vaped.
