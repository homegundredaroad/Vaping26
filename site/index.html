Why the firewall matters

The observatory is designed to make uncertainty and incompleteness visible instead of smoothing them away.

Evidence infrastructure before evidence claims

The public site is a publication surface for an audited private research pipeline. Its job is to expose state, provenance and readiness without converting automated discovery into scientific judgement.
