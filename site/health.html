Health evidence

Health questions need the right comparator.

A comparison with never-users answers a different question from a comparison with people who continue to smoke. Vaping26 is building structured evidence cards so these populations are not mixed silently.

No health verdict is generated from counts. Candidate-study counts below are triage measures. Quantitative synthesis remains blocked until outcomes, comparators, effect estimates, confounding and risk of bias have been validated.

Comparator discipline

  • never-users / non-vapers
  • people who continue to smoke
  • former smokers who do not vape
  • dual users
  • alternative nicotine products where relevant

These groups are not interchangeable. Smoking history and dual use are core extraction fields.

Study assessment

Study design is recorded, but not treated as a universal truth ranking. Synthesis should also examine risk of bias, directness, precision, consistency, confounding control, comparator quality, applicability, funding/conflicts and publication integrity.
