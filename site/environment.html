Environmental & exposure context

Ground, model and satellite context

A capability layer for distinguishing background atmospheric conditions from local observations. Vaping26 treats these sources as context unless a source-specific study design supports a stronger inference.

Attribution boundarySatellite columns, outdoor monitoring stations, emissions inventories and air-quality models cannot identify indoor vaping at a premises, retailer or individual level. They are used to characterise competing background conditions and uncertainty.

Planned fusion model

Ground observations+Weather+Emissions inventory+CAMS / satellite→Context with uncertainty

The objective is confounder/context control, not automated source accusation. Machine learning is intended for calibration, quality control, missing-data reconstruction and bounded background prediction.

Ground

Particle number and composition

Specialist UK-AIR networks can add particle-number, size-distribution, black-carbon and chemical context beyond conventional PM2.5/PM10.

Model

CAMS

Modelled background and station-optimised air-quality products can be used alongside ground measurements and meteorology, with uncertainty retained.

Satellite

Sentinel-5P and EarthCARE

Atmospheric columns and vertical aerosol structure add regional context at spatial scales that must not be confused with indoor measurements.
