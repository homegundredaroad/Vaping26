Methods

Collection is not synthesis.

Vaping26 separates source discovery, data capture, normalisation, evidence extraction, synthesis and publication. The public repository is the audited publication surface; credentials, raw records and unresolved investigative work remain in the private Research engine.

Evidence pipeline

Register→Harvest→Validate→Deduplicate→Evidence card→Source-grounded extraction→Risk/quality review→Synthesis→Publication gate

Evidence cards

Each study can carry identifiers, design, population, smoking history, dual-use status, intervention/exposure, comparator, outcomes, effect estimates, funding/conflicts, publication-integrity status and risk-of-bias assessment. Vaping26 automatically classifies only fields that can be supported from bibliographic metadata; quantitative and interpretive fields remain unfilled until source-grounded extraction and review.

Study design is not a universal truth score

Vaping26 records study design but evaluates its relevance to the question. Certainty depends on risk of bias, directness, precision, consistency, comparator quality, confounding control, applicability, funding/conflicts and publication integrity.

Conservative study-design resolution

Study-design resolution follows a conservative ladder: explicit deterministic metadata and rules first; bibliographic corroboration second; bounded machine suggestion only within evaluated scope and abstention rules; and source-checked human adjudication for ambiguous or conclusion-relevant cases. Unknown is a valid state. Reducing the visible Unknown percentage is not itself a success metric, and a machine suggestion cannot mark a record reviewed or synthesis-ready.

Human review pilot

The first governed review tranche is deliberately bounded to 40 records across the registered cessation and respiratory questions. Queue selection is deterministic and does not constitute scientific review. Conclusion-sensitive fields require exact source-location provenance, a source-checked reviewer decision and a second check before public effect estimates may be released. A reviewed evidence card is still not automatically synthesis-ready; question-specific eligibility, comparator compatibility, outcome compatibility and risk-of-bias gates remain separate.

Research integrity

Retraction/correction metadata can be checked before a record enters synthesis. A retraction flag is a publication-integrity state, not a judgement about every related study.

Bounded AI

AI may assist candidate extraction from source text where licensing permits, but must attach exact provenance and pass deterministic schema validation and human review. AI may not autonomously declare causation, safety, diagnosis, illegality or criminality.

Prevalence

Official ONS and OHID data retain geography, age, sex/category, period, confidence limits and comparability notes. Different surveys are not joined simply because their labels appear similar.

Trials

ClinicalTrials.gov records are kept distinct from publications. The normaliser captures registered outcomes, enrolment, result modules, adverse-event group counts and publication references where available so registry-to-publication reconciliation can be developed. Headline publication-linkage gaps are limited to registry references explicitly marked as RESULT; BACKGROUND and DERIVED references remain available for audit but are not presented as missing trial-result publications.

Publication

Generated public outputs are deny-by-default, checked for forbidden paths and common credential patterns, and covered by SHA-256 manifest metadata. Compact release provenance links a publication to the Research run/code and source snapshots without exposing private records.
