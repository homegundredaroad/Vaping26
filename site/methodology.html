Methodology

Evidence discipline and publication controls

Vaping26 is designed to distinguish evidence from assumption. Sources are assessed according to their role, provenance, study design, corroboration, uncertainty and suitability for public release.

The public repository is downstream of a deny-by-default publication firewall. Raw collection infrastructure, unpublished investigative material, sensitive records and security-sensitive processes remain outside the public build.

Pipeline

Register → collect → snapshot → validate → cross-reference → normalise → audit → aggregate → publication firewall → public observatory.

Evidence roles

Sources are not treated as interchangeable. Bibliographic databases support research discovery; trial registries describe registered studies; official statistics support prevalence work; product registers support regulatory-record analysis; Companies House can support candidate entity discovery; media sources are discovery leads only unless corroborated by appropriate authoritative evidence.

Health evidence

Counts of papers or trials are inventory measures, not measures of effect or certainty. Vaping26 separates study metadata from conclusions and distinguishes association from causation. A recent-first surveillance index is not described as a complete systematic review.

Environmental and exposure context

Ground-monitor networks, meteorology, emissions inventories, atmospheric models and satellite products are treated as distinct contextual evidence layers. Spatial and temporal resolution, instrument method, calibration and uncertainty are retained. These sources are not used to infer indoor or premises-level vaping emissions without an appropriate study design.

Bounded machine learning

Machine learning is intended for defined technical tasks such as sensor calibration, quality-control anomaly detection, missing-data reconstruction, evidence relevance screening and background prediction. Model outputs do not independently establish health causation, illegality, misconduct or source attribution.

High-street and company evidence

Company or location records can help identify candidate entities or geographic patterns. A retailer, company or product appearing in a dataset does not itself establish misconduct, regulatory non-compliance or criminality.

Enforcement evidence

Public claims about identifiable enforcement events should be tied to suitable authoritative records and proportionate human review. Media reports can identify leads but do not independently establish wrongdoing.

Publication controls

  • Explicit allowlist for generated public roots.
  • Credential and secret-pattern scanning.
  • Publication-manifest hashes and byte-size checks when a manifest is present.
  • Regression tests for site structure and publication safety.
  • GitHub Pages deployment only after validation and build success.

For the repository-level technical specification, see the public METHODOLOGY.md and AUDITABILITY.md files in the Vaping26 repository.
