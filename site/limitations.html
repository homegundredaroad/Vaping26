Interpretation safeguards

What this observatory does not establish

ImportantVaping26 is an evidence and research infrastructure project. Published material should not be interpreted as medical, legal or regulatory advice.

Current limitations

  • Coverage is incomplete while the source catalogue and collectors are still being developed.
  • Literature counts do not measure the strength, direction or certainty of health effects.
  • Registered-study records do not by themselves establish results or publication quality.
  • The ONS adult e-cigarette workbook is now parsed with confidence intervals and source comparability notes; other official workbooks may still require separate extraction and validation.
  • Business-search results are candidate entities, not a comprehensive vape-shop census.
  • Public product-register entries do not establish current retail availability or legal compliance in every circumstance.
  • Media results are discovery leads and context, not proof of illegality, wrongdoing or causation.
  • Geographic analyses can be affected by coverage, classification, geocoding and temporal bias.
  • Outdoor stations, emissions inventories, atmospheric models and satellites describe environmental context at their own spatial and temporal scales; they do not identify indoor vaping or premises-level sources.
  • Machine-learning outputs require held-out validation, calibration provenance and uncertainty reporting before public interpretation.

Identifiable businesses and products

A product, retailer, company or study appearing in a Vaping26 dataset does not itself establish wrongdoing, regulatory non-compliance, causation or health harm. Automated matching is used as a research aid, not as an adjudication mechanism.

Changing evidence

The scientific, commercial and regulatory environment changes. Publication timestamps, source provenance and archived outputs are therefore important parts of interpretation.

Corrections

Where a material error is identified, the project should correct the public output while preserving sufficient provenance to understand what changed and why.
