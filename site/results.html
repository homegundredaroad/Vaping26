What Vaping26 can report now.

This page separates established operational results from scientific conclusions that are not yet justified. It is populated directly from the latest approved Research export.

Results are not a single health verdict. The observatory can already report coverage, prevalence, study populations, trial-result availability and synthesis readiness. Pooled health-effect conclusions remain blocked until source-grounded extraction and review are complete.

Available now

  • Validated and quarantined literature counts.
  • Clinical-trial status and registry-result availability.
  • Trial-to-publication linkage coverage.
  • Official ONS and youth-prevalence extracts.
  • Question-specific evidence maps and source links.
  • Transparent review and synthesis-readiness status.

Still scientifically gated

  • Comparator-specific pooled health effects.
  • Risk-of-bias judgements without human review.
  • Causal claims from observational associations.
  • Safety or harm scores generated from publication counts.
