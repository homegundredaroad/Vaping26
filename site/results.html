Record accounting

Why the acquisition numbers are not one simple funnel

Vaping26 has two deliberately overlapping acquisition routes: ordinary literature surveillance and exact PMID recovery from ClinicalTrials.gov references. Trial-recovered publications can overlap records already found by PubMed or Europe PMC, and quarantine reasons are non-mutually-exclusive. The values below therefore describe auditable processing states, not numbers that should be added together.

What quarantine means

A quarantined record remains auditable but is prevented from silently becoming candidate evidence. Reasons include lack of an explicit vaping/nicotine anchor, pre-modern e-cigarette publication dates, secondary-only records and retraction notices that are not primary evidence.

Trial → publication reconciliation

The headline linkage gap is deliberately restricted to ClinicalTrials.gov references explicitly classified as RESULT. BACKGROUND bibliography and DERIVED references remain retained for audit, but they are not presented as missing trial-result publications.

Unmatched is not unpublished or invalid.It means the current governed linkage process has not reconciled that RESULT identifier to a public candidate record. Keeping those identifiers visible makes the gap inspectable rather than silently disappearing it.
