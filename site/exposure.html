Measurement priorities

Particles

Particle number and size distribution where available, not just consumer PM2.5 readouts.

Chemistry

Nicotine, carbonyls/VOCs, metals and other characterised constituents with method provenance.

Environment

Room volume, ventilation/air changes, temperature, humidity, distance and time since aerosol generation.

Human exposure

Biomarkers and clinically relevant outcomes kept distinct from emissions measurements.
