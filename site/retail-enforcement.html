Retail & enforcement

Map the market without turning leads into allegations.

Business searches, directories and media can help discover candidates. They do not establish that a physical shop exists, that it sells a particular product, or that anybody has broken the law.

Evidence roles stay separate.Companies House is candidate-company discovery. News is discovery/context. Enforcement findings require authoritative public records.

Enforcement
